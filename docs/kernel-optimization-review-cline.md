### 1. Kernel model

**Group preparation.** The driver (`build_cells_sharded_live_dedup`, `src/knn_clipping/driver.rs`) processes bins in parallel; within a bin it walks grid cells cell-major, and each grid cell's full point set forms one packed group (same bin, contiguous SOA slots). `PackedKnnCellScratch::prepare_group_directed` (`scratch/prepare.rs`): (1) collects the 3×3 directed ranges — center plus up to 8 ring cells, `SameBinEarlier` cells skipped, with an aggregate-work budget that demotes huge groups to per-query shells; (2) computes per-query security thresholds (4 plane dot8s for interior cells via `append_interior_security_thresholds`, else a ring-2 cap scan `outside_max_dot_xyz`); (3) picks a per-query hi threshold from a count model targeting `PACKED_HI_BUDGET` = 32 keys, using only the security floor and candidate counts; (4) runs a directed triangular center pass (SIMD `PointChunk8` dot8s; dots above hi become u64 descending keys in per-query `chunk0_keys`, dots in [security, hi] are only counted); dense cells (occ > 512) instead gather a per-query axis band (`dense_band_slots`), lower the coverage floor to `band_bound`, emit the whole band as chunk0, and disable tails; (5) runs the ring pass: every eligible ring candidate × every query (`dots`/`dots_pair` + `mask_gt`), retained above hi. Ring-pass dot evaluations (~192 candidates × ~24 queries per ordinary group) dominate prepare.

**Per-query frontier production.** `PackedQuery::frontier` (`packed_knn/mod.rs`) serves Chunk0 (k=16) then, after an `UnknownButBounded` probe and lazy `ensure_tail_directed_for` (re-dots the center suffix and all eligible ring candidates for that one query), Tail chunks (k=8). `emit_run` (`emit.rs`) partition-selects the top-k of the remaining keys, sorts (network ≤ 35), scatters slots, and reports `first_dot` plus an `unseen_bound` from the run's last dot, the hi threshold, or the coverage floor (`band_bound`/security, plus pad).

**Candidate consumption and clipping.** `consume_stream`/`clip_batch_source` (`run.rs`): forwarded edge-check seeds are clipped first; then per candidate: one fused `SlotPoint` load, self-skip, stamp `mark` (packed; slots are unique per query by directed construction) or `insert` (shell; re-coverage dedup), and `clip_with_slot_result_policy` → f64 `bisector_coefficients` + `clip_convex` (radial early-unchanged for bounded N≥5; SIMD distance mask otherwise; Unchanged writes nothing).

**Termination.** `GnomonicBuilder::can_terminate` (`builder/projection.rs`) caches a padded cos(2θ) threshold from the polygon's `max_r2`; it is checked per unchanged candidate mid-batch (bound = max(recomputed next-slot dot, `batch.unseen_bound`)), pre-batch in `maybe_terminate_or_advance_frontier`, and on bounded frontiers.

**Packed-to-shell takeover.** After packed exhaustion the `DirectedNeighborStream` switches to `ShellFrontier` (`shells.rs`): Chebyshev BFS layers from the home cell, scalar per-point `dot3_f32` into pending u64 keys, whole-sort ≤ 128 or 64-prefix partition-sort, batches ≤ 64, unseen bound from next-ring cap bounds (`cell_min_dist_sq`); the consumer dedups re-covered slots; full exhaustion with an unbounded cell falls to `recover_unbounded_after_exhaustion`.

**Premises I could not verify from the repository:** bit-identity of SIMD-lane dots vs scalar `dot3_f32` on all backends; the decomposition of mega's recorded ~11× candidate inflation into shell layers vs tails vs band; ordinary-regime `neighbors_processed` distribution beyond the cited ~8/cell.

### 3. Hypothesis details

#### H1 — Batch-remainder termination skip

- **Mechanism:** Within `clip_batch_source`, termination is currently checked per unchanged candidate against `complete_exact_bound(next_dot, batch.unseen_bound)`, so the remainder of a batch is walked one ~40–60-instruction unchanged clip at a time until `next_dot` drops under the certificate threshold. But when `can_terminate(batch.unseen_bound)` alone holds (the post-batch world is already certified), the only thing left to prove is that the *current batch remainder* is non-cutting. Testing that once for the whole remainder (exact per-candidate `candidate_would_be_unchanged` sweep) lets the cell terminate immediately, deleting all remaining per-candidate loads, stamp stores, coefficient builds, dispatches, distance evaluations, and per-candidate termination checks. This is exactly the predicate the timing-only `audit_directional_batch_skip` already evaluates for measurement; the recorded 500k Fibonacci audit shows 1,300,400 support tests, 112,559 hits, and zero false positives, i.e. the opportunity fires on roughly a fifth of cells with a sound exact variant behind it.
- **Current work removed:** Unchanged-clip work for the skipped candidates (load + `mark` + `bisector_coefficients` + dispatch + mask evaluation + `can_terminate` call per candidate), plus the emit-side scatter already paid for candidates that will now never be consumed (not recovered — sunk at emit).
- **Affected symbols:** `clip_batch_source`, `maybe_terminate_or_advance_frontier` (`run.rs`), `audit_directional_batch_skip` (`run.rs`), `GnomonicBuilder::can_terminate` (`builder/projection.rs`), `complete_exact_bound` (`run/frontier.rs`), `candidate_would_be_unchanged` / `candidate_would_be_unchanged_support` (`builder/clip.rs`), `BuildCounters::terminated`.

- **Correctness proof obligations:** (a) each skipped candidate is proven non-cutting by a test at least as conservative as the clipper's own inside test (bit-identical distance evaluation for the exact variant); (b) the post-batch certificate `can_terminate(batch.unseen_bound)` holds at skip time, so nothing beyond the batch can cut either; (c) no downstream state depends on consuming unchanged candidates — `emit_cell_output` derives outgoing edge checks from final cell edges, and stamp marks for skipped candidates are unobservable after termination (must be confirmed, not assumed); (d) determinism: the skip predicate must be a pure function of the current polygon and candidate dots, with no floating-point reassociation relative to the clip path.
- **Empirical risks:** The exact sweep costs nearly as much as the unchanged clips it skips (the clip's inside test *is* the sweep), so net saving may be only the dispatch/load/mark/coefficient residue — plausibly ≤1% whole-build; the O(1) support-envelope variant pays an `atan2` per candidate and its 64-sector sibling measured +17.0% instructions as a per-candidate gate (different context, but a warning); audits on non-hitting batches add pure overhead.
- **Expected workload behavior:** Ordinary fib/uniform: small but broad win (every cell has a tail; hits ~20% of cells per the recorded audit). Clustered/mega and shell batches: larger remainders raise per-hit value, but hit rate there is unmeasured. No regime should regress beyond the audit's own sweep cost, which is charged once per eligible batch.
- **Prior-work relationship:** `related`. Closed-negative "selected-neighbor constraint batches" gated *every* candidate through batched radial/sector classifiers and lost to speculation; this proposal runs one terminal sweep per batch at a point where termination is already near-certain, so the speculative share vanishes. Distinct from the queued dense-gated directional certificate (that is a per-candidate certificate for dense caps; this is a batch-level skip for the ordinary path, and could later serve as its dense variant).
- **Smallest falsifying experiment:** Pure counter arithmetic on existing telemetry: run the `timing` build on fib/uniform/clustered/mega and compute net ≈ `directional_shadow_saved` × (unchanged-clip cost) − `directional_shadow_candidate_tests` × (exact-test cost). If net ≤ 0 on ordinary inputs, the mechanism is dead without writing production code.
- **Telemetry:** Existing `directional_shadow_{checks,candidate_tests,hits,saved}`, `directional_support_{candidate_tests,hits,saved,false_positive_hits}`, `no_progress_tail` distribution, per-cell `neighbors_processed`. Minimum new counter: none for falsification; a single `batch_remainder_skipped` counter if productionized.
- **Why this is not merely a micro-optimization:** It removes whole clipping operations and candidate-consumption iterations from every cell's tail via a certificate-driven algorithmic skip, rather than substituting instructions inside the clip.


#### H2 — Retention cap with certificate-floor move ("cull chunk0 to budget, lower the floor, extend the lazy tail")

- **Mechanism:** The count model picks the hi threshold `t` from a uniform-on-[security,1] assumption over candidate *counts* only. On skewed distributions the realized dots violate the model: recorded evidence shows 86.4% of `chunk0_keys` unused at 100k clustered, 18.67M keys materialized at 100k clustered even after center-tail deferral, and a ~9.5 MiB per-worker key-capacity peak at 500k clustered (vs 6,464 keys on uniform). All candidate dots are computed anyway during the center/ring passes; the retention split can therefore be moved to the actual stream: retain only the top-`PACKED_HI_BUDGET` keys per query, set the chunk0-exhaustion certificate floor to the smallest retained dot (a *tighter* floor than `t`), and let the lazy tail cover everything between the security floor and that kth dot. Retention, key traffic, emit partition size, and live-state bytes all shrink toward the budget on exactly the distributions that currently overshoot; the improved exhaustion bound should also terminate some cells earlier.
- **Current work removed:** Retained/materialized candidate keys (~2–6× reduction on clustered per the recorded unused-key and peak-capacity figures), `emit_run` partition depth over the surplus, key-storage allocator traffic and cache footprint; possibly some consumed candidates via the tighter exhaustion certificate.
- **Affected symbols:** threshold-selection block and `self.thresholds`/`self.tail_possible` in `prepare_group_directed` (`scratch/prepare.rs`), `PACKED_HI_BUDGET` and the `PACKED_COUNT_MODEL_*` flags (`policy.rs`), `chunk0_keys`/`tail_keys` (`scratch.rs`), `emit_run`/`next_chunk` (`scratch/emit.rs`), `ensure_tail_directed_for`, `tail_upper_bound`, `resume_security` (`scratch/emit.rs`, `scratch.rs`); telemetry `observe_key_storage`, `add_unused_chunk0_keys`, `add_tail_possible_queries`, `inc_tail_requested_queries`, `add_ring_tail_rescan`.
- **Correctness proof obligations:** (a) coverage continuity: every candidate is accounted for exactly once across chunk0 ∪ tail ∪ below-security after the split floor moves — the band (t, kth_dot] must be served by the tail, so tail thresholds must move to the cull floor; (b) the chunk0-exhaustion certificate must use the exact smallest retained dot (full key order, including slot tie-break), not a model value; (c) `GRID_DOT_BOUND_PAD` conservatism preserved at the new floor; (d) band-mode (dense) paths and `MAX_AGGREGATE_CANDIDATE_WORK` slow-path behavior unchanged unless deliberately included; (e) deterministic cull order.
- **Empirical risks:** Tail-request incidence rises on clustered — documented as expensive (productive lazy rescans ≈1.5% of 500k clustered runtime; the retired lazy-recompute variant rebuilt 15.75M keys and lost 28.3% instructions, though that variant retained *nothing* above the ask); peak-storage reduction may not convert to time if retention was not cache-limiting; ordinary cells are already near budget, so the ordinary path must remain bit-identical or it risks the many pinned-codegen behaviors.
- **Expected workload behavior:** Clustered/mega: principal benefit (retention overshoot and live-state peak are recorded there). Ordinary fib/uniform: near-neutral by construction (model retention ≈ budget); any measured ordinary regression rejects. Degree-heavy adversarial cells are the tail-incidence watch item.
- **Prior-work relationship:** `related`. The lazy-recompute retirement removed *retention itself* and rebuilt on demand; this proposal keeps exact retention semantics and only moves the split point, which is what makes the certificate floor improve instead of degrade. Also complementary to the recorded center-tail deferral (which deferred only materialization of *counted* band candidates). No recorded experiment caps `chunk0_keys` at the budget with the floor/tail continuity change.
- **Smallest falsifying experiment:** Bound the upside from existing `TIMING_KV` on clustered/mega (`packed_keys_materialized`, `packed_key_capacity_peak`, `unused_chunk0_keys`, `tail_requested_queries`, `ring_tail_rescans` and dot counts): if unused keys × per-key costs are a small fraction of the kernel phase, stop. Otherwise implement the cull + floor move behind a policy flag and A/B at one clustered scale with the standard counter matrix plus tail-incidence counters; a tail-rescan cost exceeding the retention saving falsifies it.
- **Telemetry:** Existing `packed_keys_materialized`, `packed_key_capacity_peak`, `unused_chunk0_keys`, `tail_possible`/`tail_requested_queries`, `ring_tail_rescans` (with dot counts), `observe_key_storage`. Minimum new counter: none required; a `retained_after_cull` histogram is a nice-to-have.
- **Why this is not merely a micro-optimization:** It reduces three of the prompt's work categories at once (retained keys, partition/sort work, hot live-state bytes) with a documented ~9.5 MiB/worker footprint at stake, and it tightens a certificate — an algorithmic change to what is stored and what is certified, not an instruction substitution.

#### H3 — Group-shared shell traversal for band-mode dense groups

- **Mechanism:** When a query exhausts the packed stages, its `ShellFrontier` re-walks from the home cell: BFS layer discovery, per-cell visited stamping, scalar per-point dots, pending-key builds and per-layer sorts. All queries in one grid-cell group share the same start cell, the same bin, and the same `DirectedCellMode` for every non-center cell (that relation depends only on `start_cell`/cell/bin, not on the query local), so their BFS *layer structure* is identical; only per-query dots, center-slot eligibility (`allows_center_slot`), pending keys, and bounds differ. For band-mode dense groups — where takeover is the designed backstop below `band_bound` — one shared walk could compute layer cell lists and candidate slot streams once per group, with per-query dot evaluation (batchable 8-queries-at-a-time against shared candidate loads, as in prepare) and per-query bounds from the shared layers, instead of q independent full re-walks. The repeated traversal work (visited stamping, neighbor enumeration, range discovery, candidate loads) disappears; per-query state stays per-query, so same-bin cells still complete serially and the live edge-check stitching protocol is untouched.
- **Current work removed:** Repeated shell traversal: q× layer-discovery bookkeeping, q× visited-stamp traffic, q× candidate-slot enumeration and loads; scan-dot instruction count reduced by SIMD batching across the group's queries if layers are large enough to fill lanes.
- **Affected symbols:** `ShellFrontier` (`frontier`, `advance`, `scan_cell`, `build_pending`, `prepare_pending_prefix`, `CubeMapGridScratch::{pending, visited_stamp}`, `shells.rs`), `DirectedNeighborStream` takeover arm (`query/stream.rs`), `cell_min_dist_sq` (`query/mod.rs`), `DirectedEligibility::{cell_mode, allows_center_slot}` (`query/directed.rs`), group loop `emit_generator_group` (`driver.rs`), `band_mode`/`center_bound` (`packed_knn/scratch.rs`).
- **Correctness proof obligations:** (a) per-query emission sequences bit-identical to the independent walks (order, eligibility, dedup against the per-cell attempted set); (b) per-query unseen bounds identical — cap bounds are per query even when layers are shared, and `build_pending`'s f64 query normalization stays per query; (c) directed ownership preserved (`allows_center_slot` per query local); (d) takeover entry conditions unchanged (same per-query packed-exhaustion path); (e) stitching unchanged: each cell still completes fully, in directed order, before the next builds.
- **Empirical risks:** Premise risk: by design takeover "stays rare" even in dense cells (`DENSE_BAND_TARGET_COUNT` = 128 ≫ ~8 neighbors), so the walk may not be hot anywhere; recorded 1M Fibonacci evidence shows ~19/1M cells taking shells. The retired "scalar shell dot-only SIMD" experiment (6.5–8.5% slower) may generalize to this batching if layers are small. Shared group state adds cache footprint and complexity in a code region with pinned-codegen history.
- **Expected workload behavior:** Only band-mode groups on dense/mega/great-circle inputs are eligible; ordinary fib/uniform untouched by the gate. Clustered inputs with frequent `ShellExpand` stage cells would show the signal first. Mixed inputs need the gate evaluated per group, not per build, to avoid a distribution-sensitive heuristic of the kind the repo rejects.
- **Prior-work relationship:** `related`. The ledger notes group-wide shell batching "would require a corresponding stitching/scheduling redesign; revisit it only as that larger architectural change" — this scopes the sharing to read-only traversal inside one group and keeps emission per-cell, which is precisely the part that does not touch stitching. The retired per-query shell SIMD changes the granularity (whole layers × 8 queries vs one query's small scans), which is the claimed cost-model change; the "packed-to-shell attempted-slot filtering" retirement is orthogonal (this shares no dedup state).
- **Smallest falsifying experiment:** Read existing `TIMING_KV` on mega and clustered: `shell_layer_slots` vs `neighbors_processed`, the `knn_stage` cell histogram, `shell_midlayer_terminations`, and the knn/cert phase-time shares. If shell traversal is < ~5% of those builds' kernel time, the mechanism has no regime and is dead without implementation.
- **Telemetry:** Existing `shell_layer_{batches,slots,prefix_consumed}`, `shell_midlayer_terminations`, per-cell `knn_stage`, `knn`/`cert` phase times. Minimum new counter: shell scan dot evaluations per group/cell (today only pending pushes and layer sizes are counted, not the scan dots themselves).
- **Why this is not merely a micro-optimization:** It removes whole repeated traversals (a prompt-listed category) via cross-query work sharing at the group level — an algorithmic/structural change crossing the `packed_knn`/shell/driver phase boundary — not a local instruction trade.

### 4. Considered but rejected

- **Per-(ring cell, query) spherical-cap pruning of ring-pass dot evaluations.** Directly retired: "adjacent caps rarely prune; measured net loss." No changed cost model; the group-level variant prunes strictly less (larger caps).
- **Ring-cell staging / whole-ring bound skipping (defer far ring cells to a later packed stage).** Same granularity as the retired "whole-ring packed bound skipping: neutral or worse outside a narrow dense case"; the per-(cell,query) geometry that defeated it is unchanged.
- **Lazy recomputation of `chunk0_keys` (retain nothing, rebuild on request).** Retired with measured costs (28.3% instructions, 29.8% cycles on clustered). H2 differs by keeping exact retention and only moving the split point; without that difference this stays dead.
- **Batch SIMD prefilter / rolling constraint preparation ahead of the clipper (f64x4 coefficient windows, radial preclassification, 64-sector support gates, fused exact classifiers).** Closed negative 2026-07-16 (+3.0% to +17.0% instructions); the width-one path is recorded as near the cost floor. H1 avoids this by running only one terminal sweep per batch, not a per-candidate gate.
- **Packed-to-shell dedup filtering (skip re-covered slots or above-floor dots in shell scans).** Retired: "low duplicate coverage and extra branching"; the dot-floor variant has the same granularity for ordinary cells (most re-walked points are below the security floor).
- **Scalar shell dot SIMD within per-query walks.** Retired (6.5–8.5% slower); noted as a risk to H3 rather than revived directly.
- **Reuse the dots already inside emitted keys to replace the consume path's next-slot load + `dot3_f32` in the mid-batch termination check.** Pure instruction movement (load+FMA ↔ store+load) with no cache/vectorization mechanism; also depends on an unverified SIMD/scalar bit-identity premise.
- **Adaptive/group-adaptive chunk sizes (k0 tuned from previous cells' `neighbors_processed`).** Over-emission waste is only sort/scatter (~tens of instructions per cell); consumed candidates are governed by the certificate, not chunk size. Tuning knob, not a work-reduction mechanism; adds distribution sensitivity.
- **Incremental/warm-start formulation across neighboring generators (brief Q7).** The certificate walk is unchanged (it is what forces the tail), and the changed/unchanged clip mix is unchanged; no work category shrinks.
- **Lazy attempted-neighbor stamps / per-candidate stamp avoidance.** Retired (memory-layout-ideas §1): bookkeeping exceeded the avoided stores; the recorded sparse-shell evidence does not rescue it.
- **Halving the attempted-stamp width (u32→u16) as a cache/TLB play.** Real footprint argument (4 MB/context at 1M) but no recorded evidence that this table is hot (writes are L1-clustered); it belongs to the memory-layout backlog that already owns the stamp table, and removes no kernel work.
- **Group-shared dense-band gather (union band + per-query dot filter).** Roughly net-zero by construction: saved index scans and slot copies ≈ added union-filter dots; no measured pressure attributed to the per-query gather.

### 5. Cross-cutting unknowns

- **Progress-tail and consumption distributions per regime.** `no_progress_tail`, `neighbors_processed` by stage, and shadow hit rates are instrumented but their current values on the reference workloads are not recorded in the docs (only the 500k Fibonacci support-audit numbers). This single missing measurement governs H1's ceiling, any chunk-size question, and the value of every certificate-tightening idea, including the queued dense-gated certificate.
- **Mega's ~11× candidate inflation decomposition.** Not established from the repository whether it lives in shell layers, packed tails, or band coverage. It decides whether H3 has any regime at all and how much of mega H2's retention mechanism can touch.
- **SIMD-vs-scalar f32 dot bit-identity.** `dot3_f32` is documented as left-associative two-FMA; whether `backend::dot3` lanes produce identical bits on every backend is not stated in the source read. Any cross-phase dot reuse (key-carried dots feeding termination bounds, shared group dots feeding per-query bounds in H3) needs this settled per backend; H2 is immune because its floor comes from the same keys that would have been consumed.
- **Whether anything besides extraction observes the consumed-candidate stream.** H1 requires that skipped unchanged candidates leave no trace in edge-check forwarding, attempt sets, or statistics that feed correctness (e.g., contract tests pinning `neighbors_processed`). The edge-check path appears to derive from final cell edges, but this was not exhaustively verified.
- **Current `MAX_AGGREGATE_CANDIDATE_WORK` slow-path incidence per distribution.** The budget's recorded effect on clustered peak storage implies many large groups; how often groups actually demote to per-query shells (and what those shells cost) is unrecorded, and it bounds the value of extending H2's capping machinery to over-budget groups.

### 6. Recommended first experiment

**Choose H1 (batch-remainder termination skip).** It has the best information value, not the largest imagined upside: its falsification is nearly free (existing counters answer it arithmetically before any implementation), and its outcome re-ranks every other certificate-related idea, including the queued dense-gated directional certificate and the dense variant of H2/H3.

- **Reduced-work prediction:** On fib/uniform, `directional_shadow_hits` on the order of 10–25% of cells with `directional_shadow_saved` ≈ 0.5–1.0 candidates per cell on average, netting ≈ 0.3–1% whole-build retired instructions after charging the audit sweep; clustered/mega at least as high per hit, with hit rate unknown.
- **Smallest implementation boundary:** One sweep site inside `clip_batch_source` (`run.rs`), entered at the first unchanged candidate for which `can_terminate(batch.unseen_bound)` already holds: run the exact `candidate_would_be_unchanged` test over `packed_chunk[pos+1..batch.n]`; on success set `terminated` and break. The pre-batch symmetric case already exists as the audit in `maybe_terminate_or_advance_frontier`. No emit-side, prepare-side, or builder-state changes.
- **Semantic validation required:** Exactness argument (skip only proven-unchanged candidates; post-batch certificate already covers the rest) plus `cargo test --release` with emphasis on `correctness`, `validation`, `backend_fingerprint`, `adversarial`, `high_degree`, `local_rebuild_contract`, and `cargo test --profile checked`. Output must be bit-identical; only counters such as `neighbors_processed`/`no_progress_tail` may move.
- **Workloads and counters:** fib, uniform (ordinary gate), clustered, mega (density contrast); single-threaded interleaved `perf stat` per the repo's counter discipline, reading instructions/branches first; `TIMING_KV` for `directional_shadow_*`, `directional_support_*`, `no_progress_tail`, `knn_stage` mix.
- **Success criteria:** ≥0.3% whole-build instruction reduction on fib and uniform with zero output changes and no mega regression beyond noise. **Neutral:** <0.2% net on ordinary → retire the production skip and record the counter arithmetic in the ledger (the audit itself can stay timing-only). **Rejection:** any output divergence, or counter arithmetic showing net ≤ 0 before implementation.
- **What the result changes in the ranking:** If the counter arithmetic shows negligible savings everywhere, all certificate-tail ideas (H1 and the queued directional certificate) drop, and H2 (retention) becomes the first experiment. If savings are negligible on ordinary but large on clustered/mega, H1 survives only as a dense-gated variant, which directly strengthens the queued dense-certificate item and moves H2/H3 up. If H1 wins broadly, H2 remains second but its experiment should reuse the same A/B harness and counter matrix while it is warm.

- **Budget-capped packed mode for `MAX_AGGREGATE_CANDIDATE_WORK` over-budget groups (eliminate the SlowPath penalty).** Attractive and compatible with H2's machinery, but the ledger warns the fallback is a deliberate reliability tradeoff ("must not restore unbounded retained work") and the adjacent dense-band eligibility change was retired; folded into H2's future scope rather than a standalone hypothesis.
